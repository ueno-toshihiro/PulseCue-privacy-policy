PulseCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Pivot-net LLC ("we," "us," or "our") sets forth this privacy policy (this "Policy") regarding the handling of user information in the Apple Watch application "PulseCue" (the "App").

1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

The App collects and records the following health data through Apple's HealthKit framework.

  • Heart rate data (pulse rate): Used to obtain your real-time heart rate.
  • Workout data: The start and end times of your measurement sessions are saved to HealthKit (Apple Health) as workout records.

* The App accesses this data only when you have explicitly authorized HealthKit access.

2. Purpose of Use

The heart rate data and workout data we collect and record are used solely for the following purposes.

  • Displaying your latest heart rate on the App's screen and on the Watch face (complication)
  • Updating real-time heart rate data during a measurement session you have started
  • Saving your measurement sessions to HealthKit (Apple Health) as workout records so you can manage them as measurement history

3. Protection and Strict Limitations on Health (HealthKit) Data

In accordance with Apple Developer Program guidelines, we strictly observe the following limitations regarding data obtained from HealthKit.

  • Local processing only (no external transmission): Health data obtained is processed and temporarily stored only on your device (Apple Watch and paired iPhone). It is never transmitted to or accumulated on any external servers operated by us.
  • No use for advertising or marketing: We never use health data obtained for advertising delivery, marketing, data mining, or any similar purpose.
  • No disclosure or sale to third parties: We never disclose, provide, share, or sell health data obtained to any third party.

4. Third-Party Modules and Tracking

The App does not use any external analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) or third-party advertising SDKs. Your usage history and health data are never transmitted to or tracked by any third party.

5. Disclaimer (Not a Medical Device)

The App is intended solely to support general fitness and wellness and is not a medical device. The data displayed by the App is not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent, or otherwise address any disease or medical condition. If you feel unwell or need a medical judgment, please be sure to consult a physician or other medical professional.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may review and revise the contents of this Policy as appropriate in response to changes in laws or regulations or to updates to the App's features. Any revised policy will take effect from the time it is posted on this web page.
